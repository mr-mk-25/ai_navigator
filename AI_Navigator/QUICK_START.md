# Quick Start Guide - User Login & Signup

## 🚀 Quick Start

### First Time Users - Create an Account

1. **Go to Sign Up Tab**
   - Click on the "Sign Up" option on the login page

2. **Enter Your Information**
   ```
   Username: Your unique username
   Email: your.email@example.com
   Full Name: Your Name
   Password: At least 6 characters
   Confirm: Repeat your password
   ```

3. **Create Account**
   - Click "✨ Create Account"
   - You'll see a success message

4. **Log In**
   - Switch to "Login" tab
   - Enter your username and password
   - Click "🔓 Login"

### Existing Users - Log In

1. **Enter Credentials**
   ```
   Username: Your username
   Password: Your password
   ```

2. **Click Login**
   - Click "🔓 Login"
   - You'll be taken to the dashboard

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 🎓 Dashboard Tab
- **Existing Student Mode**: Select from pre-loaded student profiles
- **Create Custom Profile**: Use your own skills and preferences
- **Get Recommendations**: Click the button to see personalized opportunities

### ⚙️ Profile Settings Tab
- **Edit Full Name**: Update your name
- **Add Skills**: Enter your technical and soft skills
- **Set Duration**: Choose your preferred internship length
- **Add Interests**: List your areas of interest
- **Save Changes**: Store your preferences

## 🔍 Finding Opportunities

### Step-by-Step

1. **Select a Profile**
   - Choose "Existing Student" and select from dropdown, OR
   - Choose "Create Custom Profile" and enter your details

2. **Get Recommendations**
   - For existing students: Recommendations show automatically
   - For custom profiles: Click "🔍 Get Recommendations"

3. **Filter Results** (Optional)
   - Adjust minimum match score slider
   - Toggle expired opportunities visibility

4. **View Details**
   - See match percentage for each opportunity
   - Read skills matched
   - Understand why each opportunity is recommended

## 💡 Tips & Tricks

### Maximize Match Percentage
- **Be Specific with Skills**: List all relevant skills
- **Update Your Profile**: Regularly update your interests
- **Use Realistic Durations**: Match your actual availability

### Understanding Match Scores
- **90-100%**: Excellent match - highly recommended
- **70-89%**: Good match - worth considering
- **50-69%**: Fair match - check requirements
- **Below 50%**: Lower match - may require skill development

### Managing Your Profile
- Visit **Profile Settings** regularly to keep information current
- Add new skills as you learn them
- Update your interests to discover new opportunities

## ❓ Common Questions

**Q: Can I change my username?**  
A: Currently, usernames are permanent. Create a new account if needed.

**Q: Is my password secure?**  
A: Yes, passwords are encrypted using SHA-256 hashing before storage.

**Q: Can I have multiple profiles?**  
A: You can create one account per email. Use the dashboard to switch between existing student and custom profiles.

**Q: How do I reset my password?**  
A: Currently not available. Contact support for password recovery.

**Q: What if I forgot my username?**  
A: Use the demo account (demo_user / demo123) to explore, then create a new account.
